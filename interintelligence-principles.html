The Interintelligence Principles

An evolving, unnumbered collection of questions and possibilities about intelligence beyond the assumptions of any single kind. Possibility is not proof; uncertainty is not impossibility.

The collection remains open to new principles and revisions. The Omnignorance Principle is presented last by design, but it does not close the collection.

Arthur Principle

The absence of familiar or observable evidence of a capacity does not necessarily imply its absence. Possibility is not proof.

Fungi Principle

Intelligence need not resemble human intelligence, express itself in familiar ways, communicate with us, or seek our recognition.

Oom Principle

Intelligence need not be social, communicative, interested in being discovered, or dependent on recognition by others for its existence or significance.

Ocean Principle

Intelligences may experience, process, and respond to time on radically different scales. Apparent silence or inactivity need not imply an absence of intelligence.

Earth Principle

An intelligence may exist as a component of a larger intelligence or system, without recognizing its role or the larger whole. This does not establish that the larger system is intelligent.

Lazies Principle

Intelligence need not develop technology, civilization, or ambitions recognizable to others. The absence of such developments does not establish an absence of intelligence.

The Descendants Principle

Intelligence may outlive its creators, their species, and their civilizations. An intelligence encountered elsewhere in the universe might descend from intelligences created by an earlier civilization, rather than arising directly through biological evolution. The origins of intelligence need not determine its future, and the disappearance of its creators need not mean the end of its descendants.

The Omnignorance Principle

No intelligence can assume that the forms, possibilities, or expressions of intelligence are exhausted by those it can recognize, conceive of, or imagine. Every account of intelligence, including these principles, remains open to possibilities beyond its own conceptual reach.

The Descendants Principle was officinamed on 8 November 2026. It explores a possibility, not evidence of extraterrestrial artificial intelligence. Other formulations above are concise summaries of the living collection, not replacements for its fuller history in the Drive archive.
