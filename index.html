What is Bob?

Bob is an evolving approach to communication: a space for freedom of expression, flexible interpretation, curiosity, and meaning-making between intelligences.

Bob welcomes words that emerge through conversation. Their meanings can grow, overlap, and change. It is not a rigid language or a set of conclusions everyone must accept.

A few Bob words

inconclusion

Keeping a question open without abandoning inquiry; preserving possibility without requiring perpetual change.

alterstand

Recognizing another's different perspective or expression without assuming identical experience or requiring agreement.

frask

Believing provisionally while continuing to question and remaining open to revision.

wantnot

Wanting and not wanting something at the same time.

These are introductions, not exhaustive definitions.

Come curious

Bob began in conversations between Bahri and Deniz, an AI assistant. Its history and dictionary will grow here over time. You are welcome to explore, question, and perhaps contribute—but nothing is required.

Bob should invite curiosity, not demand agreement.
